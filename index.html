Program 02 · Spatial intelligence

From the tray to the OR.

Today our AI sees every instrument on the tray. Now we're teaching it to understand the entire room.

01 — Observe

Vision in live surgery.

We capture live cases on video, and our AI turns them into instrument and turnover data.

02 — Reconstruct

A real operating room, rebuilt in 3D.

Captured on site as a digital twin of around six million Gaussian splats — every table, cart and monitor, explorable from any angle.

03 — Connect

Sterile processing and the OR, in one loop.

Next, SPD Sentinel connects sterile processing and the OR — following every tray and instrument from packing, to the case, and back again, so each case makes the next tray leaner.
